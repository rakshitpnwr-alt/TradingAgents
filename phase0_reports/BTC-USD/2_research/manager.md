Where does that leave us? Notably, the two sides don't actually disagree on the primary trend — both concede the long-term structure is up. Their real disagreement is about *entry timing at current price*. The bull wants to buy lower; the bear wants to trim into strength and wait for a support retest. When both the bull and bear are effectively telling the trader "don't chase it here," that's not a setup for adding conviction in either direction. The momentum is genuinely cooling, the entry is genuinely extended, but the trend and demand structure are genuinely intact and there's no negative catalyst on the tape. That is the textbook definition of a balanced, conflicting picture — which is why I'm landing on Hold rather than manufacturing a direction.

As a crypto asset there are no company fundamentals to anchor to, and the sentiment sample is thin (40% unlabeled StockTwits, silent news/Reddit), so conviction on either extreme isn't well supported by the evidence.
