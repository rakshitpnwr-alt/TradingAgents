# USDJPY Technical Analysis Report — as of 2026-09-30

## Price Action Summary
USDJPY has traded through a volatile two-month round trip. From early August the pair rallied from ~155.26 (Aug 3 low) to a local peak of **160.38 on Sep 2** — a gain of roughly 3.3%. This was followed by a sharp reversal: by **Sep 9 the pair had fallen to a low of 152.90** (intraday low Sep 8), a drawdown of about 4.7 handles (~4.7 yen) in under a week. Since that September trough, the pair has staged a steady recovery, climbing back to a **Sep 25 high of 158.84** before consolidating in a 156.4–158.8 range into month-end. The latest verified close is **157.40 (Sep 30)**, with the day's range confined to 156.40–157.42 — a relatively tight, lower-volatility session compared to the early-September swings.

Net effect: price is almost exactly flat versus two months ago (157.58 on Aug 3 vs 157.40 now), but the path was a large round-trip, not a straight line. This matters for interpreting trend indicators below — the "trend" readings are currently reconciling a sharp V-shaped reversal, not a clean directional move.

## Moving Averages: Mixed/Consolidating Picture
- **Price vs 10 EMA (157.22):** Current close (157.40) sits essentially on top of the 10 EMA, confirming the recent rebound has stalled and short-term momentum is now neutral rather than strongly up or down.
- **Price vs 50 SMA (158.39) and 200 SMA (158.48):** Price is trading **below both the 50 and 200 SMA**, which are themselves converging almost on top of each other (158.39 vs 158.48) — a flattening long-term trend with no active golden/death cross in progress. The 50 SMA has been declining steadily since late August (from 160.87 on Aug 31 to 158.39 now), reflecting the memory of the Sep 8–9 selloff still weighing on the medium-term average even as price has partially recovered.
- **Interpretation:** The medium/long-term averages (50/200 SMA) still frame USDJPY as being in a **mild corrective/consolidative phase below trend**, while the fast 10 EMA shows the immediate reversal has already run its course and price is now rangebound just under the SMA cluster (~158.4). A sustained close back above 158.4–158.5 would be needed to flip the medium-term trend picture bullish again; a break below the September low zone (~153) would resume the corrective move.

## MACD: Bullish Momentum Fading, Approaching Zero-Line Test
- MACD line has moved from a trough of **-1.63 (Sep 15)** to **-0.12 (Sep 30)**, a strong momentum recovery as price rallied off the September low.
- However, the **MACD histogram has been shrinking for five straight sessions** (0.548 on Sep 25 → 0.366 on Sep 30), even as price consolidated near 157–159. This is a classic sign of **decelerating bullish momentum** — the rally that began in mid-September is losing thrust.
- MACD is still negative but closing in on a bullish crossover of the zero line. A push through zero would be a confirming signal for renewed upside; failure to do so alongside continued histogram contraction would support a stall/pullback scenario.

## RSI: Round-Trip From Oversold to Neutral
- RSI cratered to **24.7 (Sep 9)**, firmly oversold, coinciding almost exactly with the price low — consistent with the sharp early-September selloff being overextended.
- RSI then rallied sharply to a peak of **59.0 (Sep 25)**, right around the price high of that rebound leg, before fading back to **50.7 (Sep 30)**, a neutral reading.
- Current RSI at ~51 signals **no directional edge from momentum** right now — neither overbought nor oversold, consistent with the sideways consolidation seen in price and the fading MACD histogram.

## Bollinger Bands: Compression After Expansion
- Bollinger middle band (20 SMA) is **156.13**, below current price, while bands span from **152.53 (lower)** to **159.72 (upper)** — a band width of over 7 yen, reflecting the volatility injected by the early-September plunge.
- Price (157.40) sits in the **upper-middle portion of the band**, well off both extremes, reinforcing the neutral/consolidating read rather than an imminent breakout signal.
- As the extreme early-September move rolls out of the lookback window, expect the bands to narrow further, which would make subsequent breakout signals more meaningful.

## ATR: Volatility Elevated but Cooling
- ATR has fallen from a September peak of **1.64 (Sep 8)** — right during the crash — to **1.44 currently**, the lowest reading since the rally began, and now even below the early-August levels seen pre-rally (~0.94–1.0 on Aug 31–Sep 1, before the breakout).
- Volatility is normalizing but **still elevated relative to the pre-move baseline**, meaning stop-loss distances and position sizing should still account for ~1.4 yen average daily true range — wider than typical quiet-market conditions.

## Driving the Move: Base vs Quote
The early-September reversal (160.38 → 152.90 in six sessions) is too large and abrupt to be a gradual USD story; it is more consistent with either (a) a sharp repricing of Fed rate-cut expectations (USD weakness) or (b) a yen-strength event such as BoJ policy signaling, intervention concern, or a broad risk-off/carry-unwind episode (JPY strength as a funding-currency flight-to-safety). The subsequent steady grind back from 152.90 to ~158.8 by Sep 25, followed by stalling, looks like a partial unwind of that move rather than a fresh trend. **To distinguish the two legs**, traders should check: (1) US rate-expectation/Fed-dated OIS moves and any US data surprises around Sep 3–9, versus (2) BoJ commentary, Japanese CPI/wage data, or any MOF/BoJ intervention verbal signals in the same window, and (3) whether risk-asset moves (equities, VIX) coincided with the JPY spike — if risk-off, this points to carry-unwind (yen strength) rather than pure dollar weakness.

## Actionable Takeaways
1. **Range-bound bias near-term:** Price is sandwiched between the 10 EMA (~157.2) below the 50/200 SMA cluster (~158.4) above — a tactical range of roughly 156.4–158.8 is the near-term battleground.
2. **Momentum has turned neutral:** RSI ~51 and a shrinking MACD histogram argue against chasing the recovery; wait for either a reclaim of the 158.4–158.5 SMA cluster with rising histogram (bullish continuation) or a breakdown through 156 (bearish resumption toward the 152.5 lower Bollinger band / Sep lows).
3. **Respect elevated volatility:** ATR ~1.44 is still above the pre-move baseline (~0.95–1.0); size positions and stops accordingly — a standard 1x ATR stop is roughly 1.4 yen, wider than typical quiet-regime stops.
4. **Watch the zero-line MACD cross** as the next confirming signal, and watch whether price can close decisively above 158.4 (50/200 SMA confluence) or breaks 156 (20-day Bollinger mid) for the next directional cue.
5. **No fundamentals/volume signal available** — this is a pure technical read; traders should overlay actual Fed/BoJ rate-path and risk-sentiment data to confirm whether the next move is USD- or JPY-driven, per the direction-attribution discussion above.
