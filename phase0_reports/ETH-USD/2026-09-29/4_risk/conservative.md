But VIX is an equity volatility measure; it tells you almost nothing about crypto-specific risk appetite, and crypto has historically been far more reflexive to real-rate shocks than equities because it competes directly with yield-bearing alternatives with zero cash flow of its own to fall back on. The 10Y moved 28 basis points in a single week to 5.24%, mortgage rates are near three-year highs, and there's a live news narrative asking whether staking even makes sense versus a 5.17% Treasury.
